FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Still unsure? Ask us directly — we answer on WhatsApp.

What services do you offer?

We offer smart home automation (KNX, Wi-Fi, Zigbee), video surveillance & security cameras, network & Wi-Fi optimization, smart locks & access control, professional electrical installations, and lighting design — for both residential and commercial properties.

How much does installation cost?

There isn't a one-size-fits-all price. The final cost depends on the scope of work — whether it's a simple smart switch setup, a full surveillance system, or a complete home automation project. Contact us for a free personalized quote.

Do you sell smart home products?

Yes! We sell a wide range of smart home devices from trusted brands like EAE Technology (KNX), Shelly, SONOFF, and Reyee. Browse our full product catalog on the website and contact us to place an order — we deliver across Morocco.
