EAE Technology is an Istanbul-based Turkish manufacturer specialising in KNX building automation and lighting control, with its full product range designed and manufactured in-house. The portfolio covers touch panels, room control units for hospitality, DIN-rail actuators and dimmers, universal interfaces, KNX-IP routers, line couplers and KNX power supplies — all built around the KNX, DALI and TCP/IP standards. The brand serves the hotel, residential, and commercial-and-industrial building sectors, with dedicated product lines for each segment. As a KNX Association member and authorised KNX training centre since 2012, EAE differentiates itself by combining strict standards compliance with direct control over development and quality, from a vertically integrated industrial group operating across more than 100 countries.
