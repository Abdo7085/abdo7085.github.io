Tuya Smart is a publicly listed Chinese AI cloud platform provider founded in 2014, positioned as a neutral, open AIoT infrastructure that connects products end-to-end — from hardware module to cloud to consumer app. Its offering covers IoT cloud services, device-development tools, AI automation, and a portfolio of own-brand hardware including multi-protocol gateways, smart switches, sensors, plugs and bridges. The platform serves consumer brands, OEMs, system integrators and independent developers who need to bring connected products to market quickly. Its distinguishing strength is reach and openness: a global ecosystem in over 200 countries with millions of registered developers, built around interoperability — Zigbee, Matter, Thread and Wi-Fi unified under a single coherent stack.
