Shelly is a Bulgarian smart-home and IoT manufacturer founded in 2017, focused on compact devices that retrofit existing electrical installations without rewiring. Its catalogue spans smart switching and dimming, environmental and presence sensors, thermostats, energy metering, smart plugs and lighting, door locks, and professional-grade DIN-rail controllers for electrical panels. The brand serves both residential customers upgrading their homes and commercial integrators handling office and facilities projects. Its distinguishing strength is broad interoperability: devices speak Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Matter, Zigbee, Z-Wave, LAN and KNXnet/IP, so they slot natively into any standards-compliant smart-home or building-management stack.
