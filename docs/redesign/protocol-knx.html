KNX

One wired bus for lighting, shutters, climate and scenes — the open standard behind serious smart buildings.

KNX is the open, worldwide standard for home and building control. Every switch, sensor, actuator and touch panel is connected to one dedicated two-wire bus cable, laid alongside the power wiring, and the devices talk to each other directly: there is no central box whose failure stops the house, and nothing depends on the internet. Because KNX is a standard and not a brand, devices from hundreds of manufacturers work together, and any certified integrator can extend or service the installation years later.

When to choose KNXFit
  • New build or full renovation: the bus cable goes in with the electrical wiring.
  • Villas, hotels and offices: many rooms, lights, shutters and climate zones under one system.
  • Reliability first: works locally, with no cloud and no internet needed.
  • Room to grow: add a room, a sensor or a brand later without starting over.

Less suited

A quick upgrade in a finished flat without rewiring — Zigbee or Wi-Fi devices fit there better.
