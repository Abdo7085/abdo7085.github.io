The project

A four-storey villa retrofit where the client wanted every light, motorized shutter and HVAC zone to work from a single interface — smartphone at home, wall touch panel in the living room, voice when hands are full. We deployed a KNX backbone with DIN-rail actuators in the main panel, touch panels on each floor, and a gateway bridging KNX to an advanced home server for Alexa and Google control. The homeowner now runs 12 pre-set scenes (movie, dinner, sleep, away) from any device, and the HVAC schedule alone has cut winter electricity use by around 18%.

We briefed the team on a Monday and they had the main floor running scenes by Friday. It feels like the villa read our minds.
