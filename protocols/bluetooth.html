Bluetooth

Short-range radio between your phone and a device — for quick setup, and as a bridge for small battery sensors.

Standard
Bluetooth LE
Medium

Bluetooth Low Energy is the short-range radio built into every phone. In the Wi-Fi devices we install it has two jobs. You set a device up from your phone while standing next to it, before it joins the home network. And a mains-powered device can act as a bridge for small Bluetooth sensors and buttons, passing their events on to the Wi-Fi network with no extra hub. Its range is a room or two, so Bluetooth does not run a whole house on its own: it is a handy companion to Wi-Fi.

How a Bluetooth installation worksDiagram
When to choose BluetoothFit
  • Quick setup: add a new device from your phone, standing next to it.
  • Battery sensors and buttons close to a mains device that bridges them to the Wi-Fi.
  • No extra hub: the bridge is built into the device itself.
  • One small spot: a button by the bed, a sensor on a door.

Less suited

Running a whole house — the range is too short; Wi-Fi, Zigbee or KNX carry the system.
