Matter

One device, every app: Matter works with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa and SmartThings — at the same time, and locally.

Matter is an open standard from the Connectivity Standards Alliance, backed by Apple, Google, Amazon and Samsung. A Matter device is controlled on the home network, not through a maker's distant server, and the same device can be added to several apps at once, so one person uses Apple Home and another Google Home. Matter runs over Wi-Fi, Ethernet and Thread, and a bridge brings an existing system into it: a Matter Zigbee hub, for example, shows its Zigbee devices in the Matter apps. Matter is young and still growing, so it is worth checking which device types and features each app supports.

When to choose MatterFit
  • A family on different phones: the same lights in Apple Home, Google Home or Alexa.
  • Freedom to change: devices are not tied to one maker's app.
  • Local control: switching runs on the home network, not through a distant server.
  • Mixing systems: a bridge brings Zigbee devices into the same apps.

Less suited

Advanced scenes and large wired installations — Matter covers the essentials; KNX still does far more.
