Wi-Fi is the wireless network the whole house shares: phones, laptops and TVs, but also security cameras, video intercoms, wall displays and smart modules for lighting and shutters. Every device joins the home router directly, with no extra hub. For cameras and intercoms that means live video on the phone, at home or away; for smart devices it is worth knowing where the control runs — devices that work locally keep going when the internet is down, while cloud-dependent ones stop answering the app. Since every device takes a share of the network, a solid router and well-placed access points make the difference between a reliable installation and a frustrating one.

When to choose Wi-FiFit
  • Finished homes: cameras, intercoms and smart modules added without pulling new cables.
  • One network for everyone: phones, TVs, laptops and smart devices together.
  • Cameras where a cable cannot go: a garden, a gate, an outbuilding within range.
  • Start small and grow: a camera or a few rooms first, more later.

Less suited

Many cameras recording around the clock, or dozens of devices on a weak network — a wired LAN with PoE, KNX or Zigbee fit better there.
