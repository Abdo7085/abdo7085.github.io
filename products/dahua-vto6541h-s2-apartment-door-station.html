About this product

The Dahua VTO6541H-S2 is a Lite-series IP face recognition apartment door station that combines four access methods — face, fingerprint, IC card, and password — in one sleek panel. Its 2MP HD dual-lens camera with white light illuminators delivers clear identification day and night, while the 4.3-inch LCD screen provides visual feedback to visitors. The AI engine stores up to 20,000 face templates, 20,000 cards, and 10,000 fingerprints, matching faces with 99.9% accuracy in just 0.2 seconds at distances from 0.3 m to 2.0 m. Liveness detection blocks photo and video spoof attempts, and the device supports duress, tamper, and door-detection alarms for enhanced security. Built-in two-way audio allows visitors to communicate with residents, and unanswered calls can leave video messages when paired with a Dahua indoor monitor. With RS-485 and Wiegand ports, 2 alarm inputs, 2 alarm outputs, and a 12V power output for extension modules, this door station integrates easily into any access control system. The IP65-rated PC+ABS housing withstands harsh weather, and both PoE and 12V DC power options simplify installation.
