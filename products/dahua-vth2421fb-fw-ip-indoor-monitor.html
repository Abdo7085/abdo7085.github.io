About this product

The Dahua VTH2421FB-P/VTH2421FW-P is a versatile 7-inch indoor monitor designed for IP video intercom systems. Its capacitive touch screen delivers crisp visuals at 1024×600 resolution, while the built-in microphone and 1.5W loudspeaker enable hands-free two-way communication with visitors at your door. The device supports live IPC surveillance, allowing you to monitor IP camera feeds directly from the screen. With 6 alarm inputs, 1 alarm output, and SOS support, it integrates seamlessly with residential security systems. An embedded 8 GB microSD card stores call logs, snapshots, and text messages. The monitor connects via standard PoE (RJ-45) for both power and data, supports up to 9 substation door stations, and can receive and display notifications pushed from the management center. Its slim PC+ABS casing mounts cleanly on any interior wall.
