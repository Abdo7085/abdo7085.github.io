About this product

The Zennio Tecla 55 is a compact backlit capacitive touch switch designed for standard 55×55 mm frames — the most widely used format in Europe and North Africa. Available in X1, X2, X4, and X6 variants, each button lights up individually so the panel is always easy to use in low-light conditions. Built-in temperature, proximity, and brightness sensors allow the Tecla 55 to auto-adjust its backlight and run a built-in thermostat for climate control. An acoustic feedback option provides a subtle click sound on each touch. It mounts in standard 55 mm flush-mounted back boxes and is available in White, Silver, and Anthracite finishes to blend into any interior.
