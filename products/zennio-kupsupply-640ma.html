About this product

The Zennio KUPSupply 640mA is a universal KNX system power supply designed for medium to large KNX installations. It accepts a wide-range AC input (110–240 V) so it can be deployed in any country without an adapter, and delivers 29 V DC SELV with a total combined output of 640 mA shared between the KNX bus and an auxiliary 29 V DC output — ideal for powering bus-powered sensors or additional low-voltage devices directly from the same unit. Onboard short-circuit and overvoltage protection keep the installation safe, and a front-panel LED instantly signals any overload condition so faults are spotted at a glance. The 4.5 DIN-module footprint fits neatly in a standard electrical enclosure alongside actuators and other DIN-rail equipment.
