About this product

The Tuya Matter Gateway is a wired smart home hub that connects a wide range of smart devices under one roof. Supporting up to 128 Zigbee sub-devices alongside Matter and Thread protocols, it works natively with Apple Home, Google Home, Amazon Alexa, and SmartThings without additional bridges. The wired Ethernet connection ensures rock-solid stability — no Wi-Fi dropouts — while TLS encryption and local storage keep your data private. Simply plug it into your router, power it via USB-C, and start building a truly interoperable smart home.
