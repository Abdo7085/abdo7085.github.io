About this product

The Shelly Dimmer Gen3 brings smooth, smart dimming to any room in your home. Instead of just turning lights on or off, you can adjust brightness to set the perfect mood for dinner, movie night, or relaxation. It works with both modern LED bulbs and traditional incandescent or halogen lights, delivering flicker-free dimming across the full range. Installation happens behind your existing wall switch, and it works even in older homes without a neutral wire. Control brightness from the app, set schedules to wake up gently with a sunrise effect, or use voice commands through Alexa or Google Home.
